# Content Policy

This repository is public. Anything committed here is published.

## Allowed

- Material from the System Reference Document 5.1 (SRD 5.1), which is licensed under [CC-BY-4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode). Any SRD content in the repo must carry this attribution:

  > This work includes material taken from the System Reference Document 5.1 ("SRD 5.1") by Wizards of the Coast LLC and available at https://dnd.wizards.com/resources/systems-reference-document. The SRD 5.1 is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License available at https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.

- Content the repo owner wrote themselves.

## Not allowed

- Text from published books, beyond what SRD 5.1 contains.
- Material supplied by a DM (campaign notes, handouts, homebrew).
- Real names of people, or employer details.

## Private content

Private content is pasted into the app at runtime and kept in browser storage only. It is never committed.
